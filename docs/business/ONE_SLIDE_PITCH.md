## Notes for Presenters

1. **The one-sentence positioning** (top line) must remain verbatim: "The first open silicon platform for verifiable AI compute — trust in hardware, not promises." This is the category claim. Do not dilute it.

2. **The three-column center** (Problem / Solution / Proof) maps directly to the three organs of Trinity: the Problem is the world without Phi (no identity), the Solution is the Triad (Phi + Euler + Gamma), and the Proof is the submitted silicon and DOI.

3. **Performance figures** are the only authorized claim: "~1 GOPS @ ~50 MHz @ ~1 W ternary per die (projected, pending tape-out 2026-12-16)." Do not substitute alternative figures. Do not compare ternary GOPS to binary TFLOPS.

4. **No price target for TRI.** The key facts section lists supply constants (protocol, not projection) and does not include a token price forecast.

5. **The footer contact** is admin@t27.ai. Use this for all inbound queries; do not provide personal phone or home address in public decks.

6. This slide is intended for the first 90 seconds of a VC meeting or DARPA briefing. It is not intended to answer questions; it is intended to generate them. The full answers are in the companion documents listed at the top of this file.
